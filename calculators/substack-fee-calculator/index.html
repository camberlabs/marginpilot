Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Substack actually take per sale in 2026?

Substack starts at 12.9% + $0.30 per transaction with $0 monthly fee. On $5,000/mo at a $10 average order with 40% international buyers, your true effective fee rate is 17.40%, leaving $4,130/mo net.

Is Substack a Merchant of Record (MoR)?

No. Substack processes payments directly on your merchant account, meaning you remain legally responsible for tax registration, nexus tracking, and VAT/sales tax remittance.

What are the highest-payout alternatives to Substack?

At $5,000/mo gross revenue, the top net-payout alternatives are Shopify (Basic) ($4,626/mo net), Stripe (+ Tax & Billing) ($4,611/mo net), Creem.io ($4,605/mo net).

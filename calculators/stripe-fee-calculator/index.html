Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Stripe (+ Tax & Billing) actually take per sale in 2026?

Stripe (+ Tax & Billing) starts at 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction with $0 monthly fee. On $5,000/mo at a $29 average order with 40% international buyers, your true effective fee rate is 5.82%, leaving $4,709/mo net.

Is Stripe (+ Tax & Billing) a Merchant of Record (MoR)?

No. Stripe (+ Tax & Billing) processes payments directly on your merchant account, meaning you remain legally responsible for tax registration, nexus tracking, and VAT/sales tax remittance.
